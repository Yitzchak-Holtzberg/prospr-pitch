Money, but gentle.

Prospr, reimagined — a budget planner built for every kind of brain.

Prospr v1 proved the planning model works. The next version keeps that brain and rebuilds the experience around how neurodivergent people — ADHD, autism, dyscalculia, money anxiety — actually live with money. Five principles drive every screen:

looks_one

One number you can trust

Every screen leads with "safe to spend." No running math, no mental arithmetic — the app does the subtraction so the user never has to.

inventory_2

Buckets, not spreadsheets

Money lives in named buckets with an allowed spend. Groceries has $86 left — that's the whole story. No categories-of-categories, no blank grid of doom.

self_improvement

No alarm colors, no shame

An overspent bucket doesn't turn red — it goes quiet and "rests" until the next refill. Calm cream surfaces, muted sage and terracotta, zero sensory spikes.

counter_1

One decision at a time

Payday money gets placed through a guided flow — one bucket, one question, next. Executive function is a budget too; we spend as little of it as possible.

key

Nothing to remember

Passwordless sign-in (email link, Google, Apple). Remembering a password is an executive-function tax — so we removed it.

tune

Simple defaults, opt-in depth

Works out of the box with four starter buckets and a weekly rhythm. Power features (rules, custom periods, reports) stay behind a door, never in the way.
